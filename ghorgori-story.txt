Ghorghori was a little green frog who lived beside a quiet pond.

One morning, he looked at his reflection in the water and said, “Wow! What a handsome frog!”

Just then, a fish jumped out and splashed water all over his face.

Ghorghori blinked and said, “Okay… maybe a handsome *wet* frog.”

He decided to catch a fly for breakfast. He waited very carefully, stuck out his tongue, and—SLURP!

But instead of catching the fly, he caught a leaf.

“Mmm,” said Ghorghori, chewing seriously. “Very crunchy fly.”

The other frogs laughed so hard that one of them fell into the pond.

Ghorghori smiled proudly and said, “See? I’m not just a frog. I’m a comedian!”

From that day on, whenever the pond was too quiet, everyone shouted:

“GHORGHORI! TELL US A JOKE!”

And Ghorghori would answer, “Sure! But first… has anyone seen my breakfast?”
